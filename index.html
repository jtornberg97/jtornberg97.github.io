About

Hi, I'm Jonathan — a Charlotte-based product manager with a consulting background in healthcare, insurance, and financial services.

I spend most of my time at the messy intersection where business goals, engineering realities, and customer needs collide. My favorite work? Taking a bloated, legacy process and leaving it feeling like magic — for the people using it and the teams supporting it.

Today I'm a Technical Consultant at Ushur, leading implementation for six Fortune 500 accounts on contracts totaling more than $3M ARR. Before that I was a business systems analyst at CapTech, embedded with a top-3 US bank on risk and compliance transformations.

Outside the workday you'll usually find me somewhere in Charlotte's food scene, at a Panthers or Hornets game, or tinkering with something new on the side.
